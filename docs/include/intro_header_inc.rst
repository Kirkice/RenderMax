Welcome to RenderMax - a RenderDoc-compatible graphics capture, debugging, and performance analysis tool for Vulkan, D3D11, D3D12, OpenGL, and OpenGL ES development on Windows, Linux, Android, and Nintendo Switch :sup:`TM`.

This document serves as a reference guide and introduction to RenderMax, including its graphics debugging and performance-analysis workflows. Compatibility-facing APIs, capture files, and command-line tools retain their established RenderDoc identifiers.

RenderMax is derived from RenderDoc and continues to include the applicable upstream notices and licensing information.
